My Journey

When it comes to my interest in technology, it has always been driven by curiosity. I like to learn the in's and out's of systems and how they work, solve problems and finding better ways to innovate in the real world. When I first arrived at Texas Tech University, I was pursuing a degree in Electrical Engineering because I wanted to explore one of the most innovative fields behind modern technology. It challenged me to think in so many different ways that I thought I'd never be able to think before.

As I continued through my coursework, I discovered that my intersts extended beyind hardware and into software development, automation, and tech-driven solutions. I found myself enjoying the process of designing programs and improving workflows. I could understand a portion of electronic and hardware systems but I wanted to understand softwware systems more. After careful consideration, I made the decision to Major in Computing Applications.

Changing majors represented a shift in my coursework that aligned more with my interests and goals long-term. Rather than starting over, I was able to take any extensive knowledge I obtained from doing engineering and apply those principals into my coursework now.
