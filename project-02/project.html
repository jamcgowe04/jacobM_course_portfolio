Engineering Projects

Battle Bot Rover

During my time in enginnering I had the oppurtunity to develop a remote controlled battle bot that was equipped with capabilities of navigating an arena while engaging in strategic combat. It recvied movement inputs and controls from a custom remote control that communicates via Infrared signals and was powered by a 9.6 V power source. This was a team effort over a 10 week period all the way up to demo day where my team and I had to go up agaisnt other Battle Bots. My contributions involved the design and development of the Robots mechanical systems and the overall design for defense and method of attacks.

Technologies and Skills included:

  • Embedded systems
  • Microcontrollers
  • IR Communcation
  • Remote Control Design
  • 3D Modeling with Autodesk
